VELMAROX.COM - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
The current version lives in <meta name="version"> in docs/index.html and in the latest entry here.
Git history is the archive: every release is a commit, and old versions can be checked out from it.

v0.19.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- Clack Arena v0.5.0: pixel art faces and cat enemies.
  - Face icons are now pixel art from the Fantasy RPG Icon Pack by Franuka (CC BY 4.0): Strike is a steel sword,
    Pierce a steel arrow, Guard a steel shield, Mend a healing potion, Charge fire, Thorn a thorn whip, Skull a
    skull and The House's Jackpot a crown. They're drawn without smoothing on the 3D dice, the face chips, the Forge
    and the rules page. Pips keeps its plain pips. The game waits for the icons before the title screen.
  - Every enemy is a cat on the map: Pips the white cat, Glassjaw the blue-collar cat, Snake Eyes & Boxcars the
    Siamese, Tumbler the tiger, Iron Mike the vampire cat and The House the Egyptian cat. Map cards play the cat's
    idle loop; the route strip shows a still. Reduce motion (or the system setting) stops the loop.
  - Credits on the rules page and the game page.
- tools/build_arena_art.py cuts the icons and cats from the source files in art/fantasy-rpg-icons/ and
  art/all-cats-3/ (only the files the game uses are kept) into games/clack-arena/play/icons/ and ui/.
- Clack Arena for Windows goes to 0.5.0; the Download for Windows button points at the clack-arena-v0.5.0 release.

v0.16.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- Clack Arena for Windows (desktop/clack-arena/, not published): an Electron app in a frameless window. The
  Windows title bar and menu bar are off; the app draws its own pixel title bar (drag, double-click to maximize,
  minimize / maximize / close) and a menu bar: Game (New run, Title screen, Quit), View (Full screen, Zoom,
  Reload), Options (Settings, Sound), Help (How to play, velmarox.com, About). Shortcuts work while playing.
  It runs fully offline (the game, Clack Attack's dice engine and the fonts are packed in) and remembers its size.
- Installer: per-user (no admin, no "who is this for" page), choose a folder, desktop and Start menu shortcuts,
  pixel art sidebar, launch on finish. Uninstalling asks whether to delete your settings; No is the default.
- The game exposes ARENA.menu (new run, title, rules, settings, sound) for the menu bar.
- .github/workflows/clack-arena-windows.yml builds the installer on a Windows runner when the game or the desktop
  app changes; on main it publishes the GitHub release clack-arena-v<version> with the installer attached.
- Clack Arena's page has a Download for Windows button (that release). Clack Arena is the first featured slide
  on the home page and first in Trending now (PokéDungeon drops off Trending, which shows six).

v0.15.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- Clack Arena v0.2.0: a design overhaul aimed at making the game explain itself.
  - Aiming is a slingshot: press your die, pull back, let go; the arrow shows where it will go and a pixel power
    meter shows how hard. Settings can switch to point-and-drag aiming and hide the arrow.
  - The tray shows who is who: a striped yellow zone you throw from (it pulses on your turn), a red one for the
    enemy, and a ring in its owner's colour under every die.
  - Hard Clacks spark and freeze for a beat. Enemies that preview their throws show a "tries 3 throws" spinner,
    then the throws they threw away as fading red trails.
  - Each turn resolves in visible beats (Clack, Guard, Hits, Thorn) with a phase strip, numbers floating off the
    dice and health that drains one pip segment at a time. Tap the tray to speed it up.
  - Die panels show health as pips, odds bars by kind of face, average hit, and every face can be tapped to read.
  - One-time tips the first time you aim, land, Clack and see the map; a rules page; a Settings page (aim, arrow,
    sound, dice speed, reduce motion, tips); synthesized sound effects for every kind of event.
  - New map with the whole route and fact cards per stop; Forge shows odds before and after; Tavern says why you
    can't buy something; reward, rest and end screens with stars.
- GUI pieces cut from Pixel UI pack 3 (source sheets in art/pixel-ui-pack-3/, not published) by
  tools/build_arena_ui.py into games/clack-arena/play/ui/ at 2x and 3x. Pixel font: ThaleahFat, copied from
  Animalism.

v0.14.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- New game: Clack Arena (games/clack-arena/), a dice-building roguelike and its first playable build (v0.1.0).
  You are a die (a d4 called Caltrop). You and the enemy throw into one shared tray with real collisions,
  and the face you land on is what you do. Hard hits mid-tumble deal Clack damage; a die left leaning on
  another is Pinned. One run is nine stops on the Green Felt: fights, an elite, the Forge, the Tavern, a
  rest and The House. Items: Nudge, Chalk, Talc, Lead Shot. Relics: Lead Core, Rubber Coat, Snake Oil,
  Cracked Shell.
- The game reuses Clack Attack's dice engine straight from tools/clack-attack/play/Table/ (the three.js +
  cannon-es bundle, Dice_Geometry.js, the Green Felt tray picture and the dice sounds), so a change there
  reaches Clack Arena too. Its own code is games/clack-arena/play/js/.
- Game page with cover, thumb and two screenshots; games.js entry flagged "Early build".
