VELMAROX.COM - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
The current version lives in <meta name="version"> in docs/index.html and in the latest entry here.
Git history is the archive: every release is a commit, and old versions can be checked out from it.

v0.20.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- Clack Arena v0.6.0: the rules explain themselves as they happen.
  - The first time a Clack, a Pin, Guard, a Pierce through Guard, Charge, Thorns, or your own Skull or Smooth
    comes up in a fight, the turn pauses on a tip pointing at that die: what just happened and why. Once per
    device; Settings > Show tips again brings them back, tips off skips them. The first "Best of N" from an
    enemy that previews its throws gets the same treatment.
  - Face labels over landed dice show the number that will actually count, and why it changed: +1 from a pin,
    x2 from Charge, twice from Chalk. A Pinned label says which die gets its +1.

    modifiers in plain words (Clack +1, Takes -1). Tap one to read it. Relics like Lead Core and Rubber Coat
    show up on your own die the same way.
  - Clearer face text: Guard only stops Strikes, Pierce goes through, Charge doubles next turn's number, Thorns
    only fire when a Strike or Pierce gets through.
  - Rules page: a four-step "A turn, in order" panel, and entries for Guard and Pierce, Thorn, Charge, and weight
    and bounce.
  - Chips are coins: a pixel coin replaces the yellow button on the panels, the Tavern purse and the Tavern stop,

v0.15.0-alpha (2026-10-12)
- Clack Arena v0.2.0: a design overhaul aimed at making the game explain itself.
  - Aiming is a slingshot: press your die, pull back, let go; the arrow shows where it will go and a pixel power
    meter shows how hard. Settings can switch to point-and-drag aiming and hide the arrow.
  - The tray shows who is who: a striped yellow zone you throw from (it pulses on your turn), a red one for the
    enemy, and a ring in its owner's colour under every die.
  - Hard Clacks spark and freeze for a beat. Enemies that preview their throws show a "tries 3 throws" spinner,
    then the throws they threw away as fading red trails.
  - Each turn resolves in visible beats (Clack, Guard, Hits, Thorn) with a phase strip, numbers floating off the
    dice and health that drains one pip segment at a time. Tap the tray to speed it up.
  - Die panels show health as pips, odds bars by kind of face, average hit, and every face can be tapped to read.
  - One-time tips the first time you aim, land, Clack and see the map; a rules page; a Settings page (aim, arrow,
    sound, dice speed, reduce motion, tips); synthesized sound effects for every kind of event.
  - New map with the whole route and fact cards per stop; Forge shows odds before and after; Tavern says why you
    can't buy something; reward, rest and end screens with stars.
- GUI pieces cut from Pixel UI pack 3 (source sheets in art/pixel-ui-pack-3/, not published) by
  tools/build_arena_ui.py into games/clack-arena/play/ui/ at 2x and 3x. Pixel font: ThaleahFat, copied from
  Animalism.
