VELMAROX.COM - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha → -beta at feature-complete → -rc1 when launch-ready → v1.0.0 gold
The current version lives in <meta name="version"> in docs/index.html and in the latest entry here.
Git history is the archive: every release is a commit, and old versions can be checked out from it.

v0.6.2-alpha (2026-10-11)
- Shop closed for now: /shop/ shows a big COMING SOON panel (links to News and Games) and the home page's
  "From the shop" row is hidden. Switch: window.SHOP_OPEN in docs/js/data/products.js.

v0.6.1-alpha (2026-10-11)
- Clack Attack works on phones. Portrait: the tray sits on top (42% of the screen), the roll bar and log below,
  a one-row top bar, quick dice in a 4-wide grid of 40px buttons, 16px inputs so iOS doesn't zoom, Dice look
  opens under the tray so test throws stay visible. Landscape phones keep side by side, tighter.
  Touch screens: tap the tray to clear the dice (there is no Esc key). New files play/Mobile.css and
  play/Mobile.js; Dice_Tray.js laneRect only narrows the lane when the drawer overlaps the tray.

v0.6.0-alpha (2026-10-11)
