Clack Attack is the 3D dice from the Warrior Codex pulled out into an app of their own. It went from 1.0 to 1.3 in a single day.

1.1: updates

The app looks for a new version when it opens and every half hour after that, downloads it quietly, and shows a gold "!" when it is ready. One click closes it, swaps the new version in and opens it again.

1.2: painted trays

Fourteen trays got new painted artwork in place of the ones drawn in code, and the dice walls now sit on each frame's inner edge. Three new ones too: Green Felt, Magenta Felt and Nineties.

1.2.1: the updater, for real

Clicking the "!" closed the app and never swapped the new one in. On Windows, a detached PowerShell with no console quits immediately, so the swap now runs from a small script that cmd starts instead. If you were on 1.2.0 you had to grab 1.2.1 by hand once; it updates itself from there.

1.3: Forest Druid

The last tray without art got its own: a mossy forest floor with fallen leaves, mushrooms and dappled light, in a wooden frame wrapped in ivy. All nineteen trays are painted now.
