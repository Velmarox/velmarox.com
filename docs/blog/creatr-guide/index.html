CREATR is a Discord bot that gives everyone their own voice room. Join one channel, get a room that's yours, and set it up however you like: lock it, hide it, rename it, pick who gets in. Here's how it works.

Make your own room

  1. Join the ➕ Join to Create voice channel.
  2. CREATR builds a brand-new room just for you and moves you into it.
  3. Open your room's text chat (the chat bubble on the voice channel) and press ⚙️ Room Settings, or type /room.

The settings only appear for you, and only the room's owner can use them. Nobody else can change your room, not even server admins.

What you can change

  • 🔒 Lock: only people you've permitted can join.
  • 🙈 Hide: the room disappears from the channel list.
  • 🤐 Lock chat: only you and your permitted people can type in the room's chat.
  • ✏️ Rename, 👥 Limit (how many people fit) and 🎚️ Bitrate (audio quality).
  • ✅ Permitted: people who can always get in, even when the room is locked.
  • ⛔ Blocked: people who can't see or join your room.
  • 👢 Kick: remove someone from your room.
  • 🔁 Transfer: hand the room to someone else.
  • ♻️ Reset: back to the default settings.

Good to know

  • 💾 Your settings save automatically. Your next room starts exactly the way you left the last one: same name, lock, limit and lists.
  • 👑 Owner left? Anyone still in the room can press Claim to take it over.
  • ⏳ Empty rooms clean up after themselves. A room with nobody in it closes after 5 minutes, and a countdown shows in its chat. It cancels the moment someone joins.
  • ➕ Want a fresh room? Join Join to Create again any time and you get a brand-new one. Your old room stays for whoever is still in it.
🆕 New update

If you get kicked from a room, or blocked while you're in it, CREATR now sends you a private message saying so, along with a reminder that you can make your own room any time by joining ➕ Join to Create. Blocking someone who isn't in the room won't message them.
