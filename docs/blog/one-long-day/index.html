Here is one day of work, start to finish. I didn't plan it this way. It's just what happened.

The focus

Clack Attack on stream. The dice roller went from 1.3 to 1.6 in one evening, and it can now put your rolls into OBS with the same dice you see in the app. This is where most of the day went.

Before sunrise: Clack Arena

Clack Arena didn't exist the night before. By about five in the morning it was at 0.8.4. You're a die, the enemy is a die, and you both get thrown into the same tray. It has a map, a forge, a tavern, cat enemies, finishes to unlock, and a layout for a phone held sideways.

Morning: the Warrior Codex

If you read the last post, you know how the morning went. The player app opened once and never again.

It opens twice now. The window shows up after four seconds no matter what the page is doing, and a second launch brings back a hidden window. I also found out why players kept getting signed out: I have two worlds, and every time I opened the other one, the app threw their key away. Now it keeps the key and waits.

The same day the Codex also got:

  • The whole rulebook, so it can look up a rule without me pasting it in.
  • A portrait for every cat, not only the player cats.
  • Coat genetics. Kittens get their coats from their parents' genes, rolled with real dice.
  • A dice panel that shows the roll you owe on top, with one big button.

That's 6.27 to 6.30.2, and 264 tests that all pass.

Evening: Clack Attack goes on stream

This is the one I spent the longest on.

  • 1.4: the dice caught up with the Codex. Sketch dice that look hand-drawn, striped dice in six pride flags, and earlier rolls can stay in the tray so new dice knock into them.
  • 1.5: an overlay for OBS. Your roll shows on stream on a see-through page. It can sit in a corner, or it can replay the exact throw the app made.
  • 1.5.1: the overlay showed nothing if you opened OBS first. OBS tries a web address once and gives up, so the overlay is a file now and the two can be opened in either order.
  • 1.6: a menu bar with every setting in it. What a roll is for reads "Rolling to hit" in the app, the log and on stream. And the roll log works again. It had been empty since 1.5.

Yes, the log broke in one version and got fixed two versions later, in the same evening.

Late: a new game, on paper

The last thing I did was not write any code. Contract Zero is a game about one guy taking creature bounties, and the only way to win is to throw stuff into things. The whole design is written down first: twelve stages, every decision logged, and a review at the end whose job was to be mean about it.

The review's verdict: build a throwing toy first. One guy, three objects, a dummy. If throwing isn't fun after ten minutes, the design goes back to the drawing board.

Also

  • The casino games post went up.
  • I moved NotePadSupreme to a new folder, which broke "open with" for my text files. Fixed.

What I take from it

The morning felt like a lost day. Looking at the list, it wasn't. The bug got fixed, and four other things moved the same day.
