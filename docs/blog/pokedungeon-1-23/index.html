Version 1.23 of PokéDungeon changes three things: how you shop, how you get back into the dungeon, and how much a deep floor is worth.

Shops are people

There is no Shops tab any more. A shop is a Pokémon you walk up to: get within two tiles of a keeper and "F talk to…" appears over them. Walk away, or press Esc, and the counter closes. A closed shop's mat tells you which floor its keeper is waiting on.

The pier is the way down

Esc at the base backs out one step at a time and never drops you into the dungeon. One Esc too many on the way out of a shop used to send you straight down. The only way back in is the end of the pier, under the red arrow.

Deep floors pay

The old XP curve left a floor around B30F worth about a third of a level, so teams fell behind the dungeon from B10F and never caught up. A knockout now gives 8 + 3L + L²/5 XP and the rest of the team gets three quarters of it instead of half. A B30F floor is worth 0.85 of a level now, 0.69 for followers. The first ten floors stay where they were.

Defense also counts for more as you level, from 0.45 of it at the start up to 0.7. By B60F a wild Pokémon's hit used to take a third of your HP; now it is a quarter. B1F is untouched.

Faster fog

The fog of war used to repaint the whole floor on every step. Now it only repaints the tiles that changed: 0.19 ms a step, down from 2.78.
