How to Play

1

Mark your spots

Touch 4 to 10 numbers on the board. Those are your spots. Most people mark eight or ten. Quick Pick chooses for you; Erase clears them.

2

Set your bet

Bet One steps through 25¢, 50¢, $1.00 and $2.00. Bet Max jumps straight to $2.00 — the most a Montana machine may take.

3

Press start

The machine draws 20 numbers out of the 80 and lands them one at a time. Speed changes how fast they come.

4

Match to win

A drawn number landing on one of your spots is a catch. The more you catch, the more you win. The paytable on the left always shows what your spots pay — it changes with how many you mark.

5

The three coins

Press start and three coins drop onto numbers you did not pick. If the draw lands on one, you collect it — the coin spins where it sits and fills the next slot on the rail.

  • One coin lights the lure and shows a multiplier. It pays nothing.
  • Two coins and the second coin's multiplier is applied to your win.
  • Three coins on a play that pays opens Elo's Hoard. Roughly 1 in 15,000. This is the one everybody is chasing.
6

Getting paid

Wins go onto your credit straight away. With an account you also get a bank: press Bank to move money in or cash your credits out, and take $200 more each day. Out of credits as a guest? Refresh for a fresh $100.

House limit: no single play pays more than $800, however the paytable adds up. That is Montana law, not a setting.
