Clack Attack now works on a phone.

Held upright, the tray sits at the top of the screen and the roll box and log sit underneath. The quick dice buttons are bigger, the top bar fits on one line, and Dice look opens under the tray so you can still see a test throw.

There is no Esc key on a phone, so tap the tray to clear the dice.

Held sideways it keeps the desktop layout, just tighter. On a computer nothing changed.
