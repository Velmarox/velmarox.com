CLACK ATTACK - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v[Major].[Minor].[Patch] - app versioning. No pre-release stage tags.
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major = a release that changes how this is used
The number says which release you are on, not how far it is from a 1.0.
The top entry here is the app's version: Build_Exe.mjs and Release.mjs both read it.
Archived (outgoing-version) copies live in ../_version_archive/, never in this build folder.

v1.6.0 (2026-10-12)
- A menu bar, and every setting is in it: Dice (Dice look, saved looks, templates), Tray, Roll, Log (copy, clear), OBS
  and Window (Keep on top). The buttons and lists that sat in the top bar are gone; the OBS panel is now the OBS menu.
- Fix: the roll log stayed empty since 1.5.0. Rolls are written to it again, with the OBS overlay on or off.
- What a roll is for now reads "Rolling to ..." wherever the roll shows: in the result box, in the log, and on the
  OBS overlay in a box of its own. That box sits bottom centre; OBS > "Rolling to" box sits moves it to any corner,
  top centre, or hides it.
- Roll > Before what it's for, say: "Rolling to", "Rolling for", "Rolling", just what you typed, or your own words.
  One choice changes the app and the overlay together.
- OBS > Box size: the total box and the "Rolling to" box on the overlay, from 50% to 300%.
- The result box under the roll bar can be dragged taller (its bottom right corner).
- After an update, the overlay page already open in OBS loads the new one by itself. (Coming from 1.5.1, refresh
  the Browser source in OBS once.)
- Archived to _version_archive/Clack_Attack_v1.5.1/: App.js, App_Main.cjs, App_Preload.cjs, Clack.css, Overlay.html,
  Overlay.js, CHANGELOG.txt

v1.5.1 (2026-10-12)
- Fix: the OBS overlay showed nothing when OBS was opened before Clack Attack, or before the overlay was turned on.
  OBS tries a web address once and never again. The OBS panel now gives a file instead (Clack_Attack_Overlay.html,
  kept with the app's settings): add it in OBS as a Browser Source with "Local file" ticked. It keeps trying until
  Clack Attack answers, so the two can be opened in either order.
- The overlay tells the app what went wrong on its side (OBS shows no errors of its own), and the OBS panel says it.
- Archived to _version_archive/Clack_Attack_v1.5.0/: App_Main.cjs, App.js, Overlay.js, CHANGELOG.txt

v1.5.0 (2026-10-12)
- An overlay for OBS: your rolls on stream, drawn with the same dice and the same look as the tray, on a see-through
  page. Press OBS in the top bar, turn it on, and add the address it shows as a Browser Source in OBS. The page is
  served on this computer only, and only while the overlay is on.
- Show as, Fixed spot: the dice in rows in one corner, each spinning down onto its rolled face.
- Show as, Mirror the tray: the very throw the app made, played back over the whole overlay, with the tray's edges
  as its edges. "Hold the tray to the overlay's shape" keeps the window's tray the shape of your OBS source, so the
  mirror fills it edge to edge; off, the window is any shape and the tray is fitted inside the source.
- The total sits beside the dice in a see-through tile, with what was rolled under it. A new roll takes its place.
- Dice stay: until the next roll, or fade as they do in the app. Corner: any of the four.
- The overlay makes no sound (OBS would hear the dice twice) and draws no tray, only the dice and their shadows.
- New files: Overlay.html, Overlay.js. Build_Exe.mjs packs them.
- Archived to _version_archive/Clack_Attack_v1.4.0/: App_Main.cjs, App_Preload.cjs, App.js, Clack.css, Build_Exe.mjs,
  Table/Scripts/Dice_Tray.js, CHANGELOG.txt

v1.4.0 (2026-10-12)
- The dice catch up with the Warrior Codex (v6.30.2). Dice only: the trays, the roll bar and the log are unchanged.
- Sketch finish: a drawing of a die, in flat colour with inked edges, hatching on its shaded side and an ink outline
  round it. Three Sketch looks (Sketch, Sketch Inked, Sketch Rose) and a handwritten number font (Hand).
- Stripes: bands of colour across the whole die (Dice look > Look > Stripes), with six striped looks: Pride,
  Transgender, Nonbinary, Agender, Genderfluid and Genderqueer.
- Number outline (Dice look > Numbers): Automatic, Always or Never.
- Keep earlier rolls out (Dice look > Throw): up to three rolls stay in the tray and new dice knock against them.
  On by default.
- Face icons sit further inside their face; a custom picture is sized by its farthest corner, so it never spills over
  an edge.
- Archived to _version_archive/Clack_Attack_v1.3.0/: Table/Scripts/Core.js, Dice_Geometry.js, Dice_Look.js,
  Dice_Portrait.js, Dice_Presets.js, Dice_Tray.js, CHANGELOG.txt
